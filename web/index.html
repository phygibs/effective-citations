    How the score works

    Each direct citation earns one point. A citing paper also passes back the selected fraction of its own effective score. Separate routes add up, and the calculation follows every eligible route to its end.

    The calculator uses precomputed scores from an INSPIRE-HEP snapshot. The date rule counts a link when the citing paper is clearly dated later. Title search uses the live INSPIRE catalog, so some search results may be newer than the snapshot.
